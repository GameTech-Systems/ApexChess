# Code of Conduct

Apex Chess depends on open, fair discussion. These rules apply in Discussions, Issues, pull requests, and any official Apex Chess space.

## Expected

- Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give reasons and evidence for rule proposals, and be open to testing results.
- Report playtest and simulation results honestly, including results you dislike.
- Welcome newcomers and be patient with questions.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or threats.
- Manipulating discussions or data: sockpuppet accounts, vote or test-result manipulation, or fabricated reports.
- Spam, advertising, or posting other people's private information.
- Posting creative material you do not have the right to share.

## Enforcement

The project owner may remove content, close threads, and restrict or ban participation. If you see a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" option, or contact the project once a public contact address is published here.

## Scope and credit

This code applies to everyone participating in project spaces. It is written for this project and inspired by common community codes of conduct.
