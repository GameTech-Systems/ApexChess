# Apex Chess v1.0.0-beta.1

The first beta of Apex Chess at [apexchess.icu](https://apexchess.icu): chess, shifted 45°.

## Rules 1.0

The rules are now ruleset `standard` **1.0.0**, official and set for beta testing. They are the same rules as draft 0.1.0; publishing them as 1.0 fixes them, so any change ships as a new version. Draft 0.1.0 is retired: games recorded under it still replay and count, and rooms or events started before the switch can finish, but new rooms and events use 1.0.0.

## What you can do

- Play the computer, a friend on one screen, or an opponent online (quick match or a room link).
- Bring your own arrangement (Player's Choice); online, it stays hidden until the game begins.
- Play with a clock, share setups and finished games by link, and play offline after your first visit.
- Sign in with a passkey (no email) for ratings and opt-in leaderboards, and run private events by link.

## Beta notes

- Ratings, leaderboards and events are marked Beta and may be reset or change.
- Computer difficulty names are not strength claims until testers have played each level.
- Not built yet: the interactive rules tutorial (S32) and PGN export (S33).
- No advertising and no analytics. Feedback is voluntary: [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/GameTech-Systems/ApexChess/discussions) or the address on the About page.
